Words in context
Multiple choice
Same type as R&W M1 Q1

The committee's final report was anything but ______: it ran to just four pages and left most of the technical questions for future study.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

Solution · C

The report was short and left most questions for later, so it was incomplete. “Anything but” flips the blank, so the blank means complete: comprehensive. The report was concise and preliminary, so “anything but concise/preliminary” contradicts the text.

Command of evidence (textual)
Multiple choice
Same type as R&W M1 Q10

Biologist Rena Takeda hypothesizes that the bright coloring of a species of tree frog mainly warns predators that the frog is toxic, rather than helping the frogs attract mates.

Which finding, if true, would most directly support Takeda's hypothesis?

Solution · B

The right evidence must support “warning predators” and rule out “attracting mates.” Choice B does both. Choice A fits either explanation, C is irrelevant, and D supports the explanation Takeda rejects.

3
Inferences
Multiple choice
Same type as R&W M1 Q15

A painting long attributed to the artist Mira Solberg is signed and dated 1888. Records kept by Solberg's paint supplier show that she first purchased a particular blue pigment in 1892, yet that pigment appears throughout the painting's sky. If the supplier's records are accurate, it is most reasonable to conclude that ______

Which choice most logically completes the text?

Solution · D

The pigment wasn't available to her until 1892, so the sky must have been painted (or repainted) after 1888. Choice A is too strong: she could still have painted the rest of the work. Choices C and D contradict or go beyond the text.

Linear equations
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M1 Q4
If \(4-3x=x-20\), what is the value of \(x\)?

Solution · A

Add \(3x\) and 20 to both sides: \(24=4x\), so \(x=6\). Check: \(4-18=-14\) and \(6-20=-14\) ✓. (\(-6\) is the sign-error trap.)

5
Similar triangles
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M1 Q20
In triangle \(ABC\), point \(D\) is on \(\overline{AB}\) and point \(E\) is on \(\overline{AC}\) so that \(\overline{DE}\parallel\overline{BC}\). If \(AD=6\), \(DB=9\), and \(DE=4\), what is the length of \(\overline{BC}\)?

Solution · C

\(AB=6+9=15\). Scale factor \(=\frac{AD}{AB}=\frac{6}{15}=\frac25\), so \(BC=4\times\frac52=10\). (6 is the trap from using \(\frac{DB}{AD}\).)

Conditional probability
Fill-in
Same type as Math M2 Q3
Of the members of a club, 60% are adults and the rest are children. Of the adults, 25% volunteer at events. Of the children, 40% volunteer at events. If a member who volunteers is selected at random, what is the probability that the member is an adult? (Enter a fraction or a decimal.)

Solution · 15/31 ≈ 0.4839

Out of 1,000 members: 600 adults, of whom 150 volunteer; 400 children, of whom 160 volunteer. Volunteers \(=310\). Probability \(=\frac{150}{310}=\frac{15}{31}\approx0.484\).

Arc length
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M2 Q5
A circle has center \(O\) and a radius of 9. Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on the circle, and chord \(\overline{AB}\) has length 9. What is the length of minor arc \(AB\)?

Solution · D

\(OA=OB=AB=9\), so triangle \(OAB\) is equilateral and the central angle is \(60^\circ\). Arc \(=\frac{60}{360}\times18\pi=3\pi\).

Sum and product of roots
Fill-in
Same type as Math M2 Q8
The solutions to \(x^2-6x+4=0\) are \(r\) and \(s\). What is the value of \(r^2+s^2\)?

Solution · 28

\(r+s=6\) and \(rs=4\). \(r^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rs=36-8=28\).

Rational equations
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M2 Q14
\[\frac{2x}{x-2}-\frac{4}{x-2}=2\] How many solutions does the equation above have?

Solution · D

Combine: \(\frac{2x-4}{x-2}=\frac{2(x-2)}{x-2}=2\) for every \(x\neq2\). The equation is true for every allowed value of \(x\), so there are infinitely many solutions. Only \(x=2\) is excluded. Compare with M2 Q14 on the test, where the left side simplified to 1 and the answer was zero.

In a right triangle, the measures of the two acute angles are \((2x+14)^\circ\) and \((3x-4)^\circ\). What is the value of \(x\)?

Solution · B

\((2x+14)+(3x-4)=90\), so \(5x+10=90\) and \(x=16\). Check: \(46^\circ+44^\circ=90^\circ\) ✓. (15.2 and 18.8 come from dropping one of the constants.)

Quadratic vertex
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M2 Q17
The vertex of the graph of \(y=x^2+bx+20\), where \(b\) is a positive constant, lies on the line \(y=-5\). What is the value of \(b\)?

Solution · C

The vertex's \(y\)-value is \(20-\frac{b^2}{4}=-5\), so \(\frac{b^2}{4}=25\), \(b^2=100\), and \(b=10\). (100 is \(b^2\), the trap.)

Volume
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M2 Q20
The radius of a right circular cone is increased by 10%, and its height is decreased by 10%. How does the volume of the new cone compare with the volume of the original cone?

Solution · D

\(V=\frac13\pi r^2h\). New volume factor: \(1.1^2\times0.9=1.21\times0.9=1.089\), which is 8.9% greater. (“1% less” comes from \(1.1\times0.9\), forgetting to square the radius.)

Polynomial roots
Fill-in
Same type as Math M2 Q21
\[p(x)=3x^3-4x^2-5x+2\] The value of \(p(2)\) is 0. What is the sum of the other two solutions to \(p(x)=0\)? (Enter a fraction or a decimal.)

Solution · −2/3

The sum of all three roots is \(-\frac ba=\frac43\). The other two add to \(\frac43-2=-\frac23\). Check: \(p(x)=(x-2)(3x-1)(x+1)\), with roots \(\frac13\) and \(-1\).

15
Infinitely many solutions
Multiple choice
Same type as Math M2 Q22
\[ax+by=9\]\[3x+6y=27\] In the system of equations above, \(a\) and \(b\) are constants. If the system has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of \(a+b\)?

Solution · C

\(\frac{9}{27}=\frac13\), so the first equation is the second times \(\frac13\): \(a=1\) and \(b=2\). Then \(a+b=3\). (9 comes from copying \(3+6\) without scaling.)
