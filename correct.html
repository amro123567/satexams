Math Module 1 · Question 12
Algebra · Linear inequalities
His answer: C · Correct: D
Which of the following values of \(x\) satisfies the inequality \(2x-5>9\)?
A\(5\)
B\(6\)
C\(7\)His answer
D\(8\)Correct answer

Why his answer is wrong

He solved the inequality correctly and got \(x>7\), then chose 7. With a strict inequality (\(>\)), the boundary number is not included: at \(x=7\), \(2(7)-5=9\), and \(9>9\) is false.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Add 5 to both sides: \(2x>14\).
  2. Divide by 2: \(x>7\).
  3. The only choice greater than 7 is 8.

Remember

With \(>\) or \(<\), always test the boundary value. If both sides come out equal, it's wrong.

Math Module 1 · Question 16
Advanced Math · Radical equations
His answer: 4 · Correct: 9
\[\sqrt{x+7}=x-5\] What is the solution to the equation above?
His answer4
Correct answer9

Why his answer is wrong

4 does not satisfy the equation: \(\sqrt{11}\approx3.3\) on the left and \(-1\) on the right. The most common slip here is squaring \((x-5)\) incorrectly, and plugging the answer back in would have caught it.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Square both sides: \(x+7=(x-5)^2=x^2-10x+25\).
  2. Rearrange: \(x^2-11x+18=0\), so \((x-9)(x-2)=0\).
  3. Check \(x=9\): \(\sqrt{16}=4\) and \(9-5=4\) ✓.
  4. Check \(x=2\): \(\sqrt9=3\) but \(2-5=-3\) ✗. This is an extraneous solution.
  5. The answer is 9.

Remember

A square root is never negative, so \(x-5\ge0\), which means \(x\ge5\). Always check every solution of a radical equation in the original equation.

Math Module 2 · Question 1
Algebra · Linear equations in one variable
His answer: C · Correct: B
\[3(x-4)=2(x+1)\] What value of \(x\) is the solution to the equation above?
A\(10\)
B\(14\)Correct answer
C\(13\)His answer
D\(2\)

Why his answer is wrong

13 is exactly what you get from \(3x-12=2x+1\): the 2 was multiplied by \(x\) but not by the 1.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Distribute both sides: \(3x-12=2x+2\).
  2. Subtract \(2x\) and add 12: \(x=14\).
  3. Check: \(3(14-4)=30\) and \(2(14+1)=30\) ✓.

Remember

The number outside the parentheses multiplies every term inside. Then plug the answer back in (about 10 seconds): \(13\) gives \(27\neq28\).

Math Module 2 · Question 16
Geometry and Trigonometry · Arc length
His answer: C · Correct: A
A circle has a circumference of \(36\pi\) centimeters. What is the length, in centimeters, of an arc of the circle with a central angle of \(40^\circ\)?
A\(4\pi\)Correct answer
B\(2\pi\)
C\(8\pi\)His answer
D\(9\pi\)

Why his answer is wrong

\(8\pi\) is double the answer: it comes from using 36 as the radius. From \(2\pi r=36\pi\), the radius is 18, and 36 is the diameter.

Step-by-step solution

  1. An arc is a fraction of the circumference: \(\dfrac{40^\circ}{360^\circ}=\dfrac19\).
  2. Arc length \(=\dfrac19\times36\pi=4\pi\).
  3. Radian check: \(40^\circ=\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\), and \(s=r\theta=18\cdot\dfrac{2\pi}{9}=4\pi\) ✓.

Remember

When the circumference is given, you don't need the radius: arc \(=\dfrac{\theta}{360^\circ}\times\) circumference.

Math Module 2 · Question 17
Advanced Math · Function notation
His answer: 4 · Correct: 1
The function \(f\) is defined by \(f(x)=x^2-4x\). For what value of \(a\) is \(f(a)=f(a+2)\)?
His answer4
Correct answer1

Why his answer is wrong

4 is a zero of \(f\) (\(f(4)=0\)), but \(f(6)=12\), so \(f(4)\neq f(6)\).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Algebra: \(a^2-4a=(a+2)^2-4(a+2)=a^2-4\).
  2. So \(-4a=-4\) and \(a=1\).
  3. Check: \(f(1)=-3\) and \(f(3)=-3\) ✓.

Remember

Symmetry shortcut: the parabola's axis is \(x=-\frac{b}{2a}=2\). Two inputs with equal outputs are equally far from the axis, so \(a\) and \(a+2\) are 1 and 3.

Math Module 2 · Question 19
Advanced Math · Equivalent forms of quadratics
His answer: A · Correct: B
The function \(f\) is defined by \(f(x)=x^2-8x+7\). Which of the following equivalent forms of \(f(x)\) displays the minimum value of \(f\) as a constant or coefficient?
A\((x-1)(x-7)\)His answer
B\((x-4)^2-9\)Correct answer
C\((x+4)^2-9\)
D\(x(x-8)+7\)

Why his answer is wrong

Factored form shows the zeros (1 and 7), not the minimum. The minimum appears as \(k\) in vertex form \(a(x-h)^2+k\).

Step-by-step solution

  1. Complete the square: half of \(-8\) is \(-4\), and \((-4)^2=16\).
  2. \(x^2-8x+16-16+7=(x-4)^2-9\).
  3. \((x-4)^2\ge0\), so the minimum value is \(-9\), at \(x=4\).
  4. Choice C has the wrong sign: \((x+4)^2\) puts the vertex at \(x=-4\).

Remember

Standard form shows the \(y\)-intercept, factored form shows the zeros, and vertex form shows the minimum or maximum.

Math Module 2 · Question 20
Geometry and Trigonometry · Similar triangles — area
His answer: A · Correct: D
Triangle \(ABC\) is similar to triangle \(DEF\), where \(\overline{AB}\) corresponds to \(\overline{DE}\). If \(AB=6\), \(DE=15\), and the area of triangle \(ABC\) is 20, what is the area of triangle \(DEF\)?
A\(50\)His answer
B\(75\)
C\(300\)
D\(125\)Correct answer

Why his answer is wrong

\(50=20\times2.5\): the length ratio was applied to the area. Areas scale by the square of the length ratio.

Step-by-step solution

  1. Scale factor: \(k=\dfrac{15}{6}=2.5\).
  2. Area ratio: \(k^2=6.25\).
  3. Area of \(DEF=20\times6.25=125\).

Remember

Lengths scale by \(k\), areas by \(k^2\), and volumes by \(k^3\).

Four rules to remember

  1. Plug it back in. Check every answer in the original question (about 10 seconds). This would have saved 4 of these 7 points.
  2. Lengths × k, areas × k², volumes × k³. Find the radius before using a formula that needs it.
  3. Vertex form shows the minimum or maximum; factored form shows the zeros. Equal outputs mean the inputs are mirror images around the axis.
  4. Check radical solutions. A square root can never equal a negative number.
